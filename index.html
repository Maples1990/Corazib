FAQ

Helpful answers before you reach out

What products do you offer?

Corazon Creative Co. offers custom shirts, sweaters, cups, tumblers, vinyl decals, stickers, table toppers, and event décor for adults and youth.

Can I order for a business or a team?

Yes. Business merch, team apparel, branded cups, decals, ministry groups, and event orders are all a good fit.

What if I only have an idea and not a finished design?

That is completely fine. We can work from a rough concept, message, inspiration image, or occasion.

Can I send reference images or logos?

Yes. After submitting the form, we can follow up so you can send logos, screenshots, or inspiration photos.

How do I get an exact quote?

Use the order form with garment type, quantity, and design details. Exact quotes depend on the project.

Will someone contact me after I submit?

Yes. Your submission is received directly and we will follow up to confirm details, timing, and next steps.
