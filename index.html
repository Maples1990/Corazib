FAQ

Helpful answers before you reach out

Do you make shirts and sweaters?

Yes. Corazon Creative Co. offers custom shirts and sweaters for adults and youth.

Can I order for a business or a team?

Yes. Business merch, team apparel, ministry groups, and event orders are all a good fit.

What if I only have an idea and not a finished design?

That is completely fine. Maria can work from a rough concept, message, inspiration image, or occasion.

Yes. After submitting the form, Maria can follow up so you can send logos, screenshots, or inspiration photos.

Use the order form with garment type, quantity, and design details. Exact quotes depend on the project.

Will someone contact me after I submit?

Yes. Maria receives the submission directly and can follow up to confirm details, timing, and next steps.
