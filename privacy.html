Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Who We Are

Corazon Creative Co. ("we," "us," or "our") is a custom apparel design business operated by Maria. This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use our website at corazoncreativeco.org.

What We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide through our forms:

  • Order form: Name, email, phone number (optional), apparel details, design preferences, and any images you upload.
  • Contact form: Name, email, subject, and message.
  • Order tracking: Email address (to look up your order status).

Anonymous Analytics

We collect basic, anonymous page-view data (page visited, referrer source, and date) to understand how visitors use the site. We do not use third-party tracking services, cookies for tracking, or any personally identifiable analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your custom apparel orders.
  • To respond to your questions and messages.
  • To send order status updates via email.

Who We Share It With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only with:

  • Email service (Gmail/Google): To send transactional emails (order confirmations, status updates).
  • Cloudflare: Our DNS and CDN provider, which may process traffic data per their privacy policy.

File Uploads

Images you attach to orders are stored securely on our server and used only for your order. They are accessible only to Maria through the admin dashboard.

Data Retention

We keep order and contact data for as long as needed to fulfill your request and maintain our records. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at maria@corazoncreativeco.com.

Security

We use HTTPS encryption, secure server infrastructure, and access controls to protect your information. Admin access requires email-based authentication.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email maria@corazoncreativeco.com.
